How big is that number?

Molar volume method: 1 mol of gas takes up 24 dm³ at room temperature (about 20 °C) and 1 atm. Ideal gas law: n = PV ÷ RT. Dry air by volume: N₂ 78.08%, O₂ 20.95%, Ar 0.93%, CO₂ about 0.042%. The simulator assumes each person uses 0.25–1.4 L of O₂ a minute (resting to exercise) and breathes out 0.85 L of CO₂ for every 1 L of O₂. The speed figures use the kinetic theory of gases.
